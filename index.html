What We Treat

Pest control for homes and small businesses.

Ants

Treatment for common household ants, targeting trails and nesting areas in and around your home or business.

Spiders

Interior and exterior treatments to reduce spiders and the webs they leave around eaves, windows and entryways.

Wasps & Hornets

Treatment and removal of wasp and hornet nests around eaves, decks, sheds and outdoor living spaces.

Mice & Rodents

Targeted rodent control to help keep mice and other rodents out of your home or business.

Earwigs

Treatment for earwig activity that often finds its way indoors through cracks and foundation gaps.

Seasonal Perimeter Treatments

Barrier treatments around the exterior of your home or small business to help stop pests before they get inside.
